## Access to buildings and systems {#access}

- Access to Building and Computers
  - Obtain Amsterdam UMC Account: your supervisor will ask you to fill out a form and share a copy of your ID/passport. 
  - Obtain building card access: when your Amsterdam UMC account is ready, you will receive instructions by email from the ANW secretariat.
    - You can pick up your Amsterdam UMC staff card at the VUmc hospital (Service point, floor -1). Specifically mention that you need access to the O2 lab building.
    - Afterward, visit the VU main building (De Boelelaan 1105), room KC-06 (basement, near the securitydesk) to **encode** the staff card for O2 access.
    - See the introductory email by the secretariat for all details. 
  - Obtain a Helios server account: read more about that [here](./resources-server.md). You can download MobaXTerm to access Helios [here](https://mobaxterm.mobatek.net/download.html).

## Training {#training}

- Complete your e-learnings and MijnHR Bekwaamheden (Mandatory Skills). See explanation from the secretariat in their introductory email to you.
- If you're working on the Helios server, ask your daily supervisor to give you a tour of the server and to discuss do's and don'ts.
  - You can read more about software that you may need for your project [here](./resources-running-analyses.md).
- Discuss project-specific training with your daily supervisor.
  - If you will work with patients, you will need to perform a Good Clinical Practice training. More information and to subscribe can be found on the [Amsterdam UMC Sharepoint](https://amsterdamumc.sharepoint.com/sites/CMC1/SitePages/BROK_WMO-GCP%20cursussen.aspx).
  - We have a department-wide Datacamp license for e-learnings on statistical software and coding. Discuss access with your daily supervisor.
