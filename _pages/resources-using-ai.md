# About using AI
> _AI may enhance efficiency and learing, it must **not** replace the researcher's responsibility for accuracy, ethics, and compliance_

## Purpose of this page

This page guides the responsible use of AI tools in Team Neuropsychiatry. It applies to all staff, students, and affiliates using AI (such as large language models (i.e. ChatGPT) and code assistants) in work-related activities. 

The goal is to: 

-> Use AI as a **supporting tool** for research and professional development

-> Always protect **privacy, data security** and **intellectual property**

-> Maintain **research quality** and **scientific integrity**

## Key principles of AI usage
_Below are the principles we uphold as a team regarding the use of AI_

**1. Privacy and dataprotection**

This might even be the most important one. **Always** handle personal, confidential, and sensitive research data with care. If you need to use an AI tool to analyze research data or confidential/sensitive information, discuss this first with your supervisor and make sure they are on board. Only choose a tool that meet the Amsterdam UMC privacy and security requirements. **AI Proeftuin** of the Amsterdam UMC is an in-house approved tool. 

**2. Quality and Integrity**

Before using AI-assisted/-generated text, code and analyses, take time to review the outputs critically. Review the outputs with literature, established methods, experts on the subject (e.g. your supervisor, a statistician, etc.), and your own expertise/common sense.
