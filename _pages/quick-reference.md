## Things you can figure out yourself

**1: How to get started with your internship**
*Question:* Questions about permissions for the building, how to use the printer, e-learnings, log-in information
*How:* See the e-mail that the secretariat sent to your personal e-mail address before the start of the internship. If you have never received the e-mail, ask your supervisor first, they (or you) may ask the secretariat (13th floor, next to the pantry) if still unclear.

**2: Computer and IT**
*Question:* My computer isn't working, new badge, problems with installing Oneview
*Who to ask:* IT Support
*How:* Servicedesk via Intranet or stop by IT in the VUMC main building (-1)

**3: Software**
*Question:* I need software installed that is not already installed
*Who to ask:* IT Support
*How:* Servicedesk via Intranet 

**4: Luna**
*Question:* Where can I find more information about how Luna works?
*How:* In the Teams

**5: Declaration**
*Question:* Where can I find the declaration form?
*How:* Intranet Amsterdam UMC website.
