## About Datacamp
[Datacamp](https://www.datacamp.com) is an online learning platform focused on data and AI skills. It teaches programming languages like Python, R, and SQL through interactive, in-browser courses, along with statistics, machine learning — aimed at learners ranging from complete beginners to working professionals. A lot of the software packages thought here directly are also used by our team. 

The Department of Anatomy & Neurosciences has a license for Datacamp. You can sign up via the link on our [Team's page](
    https://amsterdamumc.sharepoint.com/sites/Div5_TeamNeuropsychiatry_SWG/_layouts/15/guestaccess.aspx?share=IQBMWRXw3Af1Q5_29SHNs8sbAZkvGe5YSypi5PZHnlZnvSY&e=SlWx1q). (Ask your supervisor for access). You will have to use your @amsterdamumc.nl e-mail.
