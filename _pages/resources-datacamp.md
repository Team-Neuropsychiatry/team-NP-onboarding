## About Datacamp
Datacamp is an online learning platform focused on data and AI skills. It teaches programming languages like Python, R, and SQL through interactive, in-browser courses, along with statistics, machine learning — aimed at learners ranging from complete beginners to working professionals. A lot of the software packages thought here directly are also used by our team. 

The Department of Anatomy & Neurosciences has a license for Datacamp. You can sign up via the link on our [Team's page](https://amsterdamumc.sharepoint.com/sites/Div5_TeamNeuropsychiatry_SWG/Gedeelde%20documenten/General/Documents/Datacamp%20license%20link.url?d=w73f8dc74aad34b6fa76723a6ab7f99f0&csf=1&web=1&e=n7pTil). (Ask your supervisor for access). You will have to use your @amsterdamumc.nl e-mail.
